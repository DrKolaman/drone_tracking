# drone_tracking

Detection & precision tracking of a small moving target in top-down aerial/drone
footage (`data/source.mp4`), under a moving camera and scene discontinuities.

See **`docs/REQUIREMENTS.md`** for the full project requirements, including the
source-video content timeline and the key discontinuities (the 647 zoom, the
745/957 jumps, and the B/W↔red colour switches).
